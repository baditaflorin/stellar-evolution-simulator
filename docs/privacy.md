# Privacy

Stellar Evolution Simulator v1 does not collect analytics, personal data, cookies, accounts, or telemetry.

The app stores only small local preferences in your browser, such as the last selected star mass and selected chart view. Those values stay on your device and can be cleared by clearing site data for https://baditaflorin.github.io/stellar-evolution-simulator/.

The app links to:

- https://github.com/baditaflorin/stellar-evolution-simulator
- https://www.paypal.com/paypalme/florinbadita

Those external sites have their own privacy policies.
